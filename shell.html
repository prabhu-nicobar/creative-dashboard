Pulled live from Meta Ads Manager. Dynamic catalogue ads are excluded from scoring and appear only as a benchmark. Every score is relative to the other creatives in the same category and audience group — never across groups. Creative quality is confounded with product demand: a good creative for a slow-selling product will score badly, so read these alongside what you know about the product. Nothing here is causal; Meta directs impressions toward likely converters, so a well-funded creative receives a better audience and looks better for reasons unrelated to the work.
